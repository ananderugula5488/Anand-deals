Our principles

A low price is not automatically a good deal. We want useful information, transparent limitations and sensible verification.

Price matters

We focus on actual price information rather than relying only on advertised discount percentages.

Context matters

Product availability, seller information, offers and other conditions can affect the real value of a deal.

Transparency matters

Prices and availability can change. Information should always be verified on the retailer's website before purchasing.

Important shopping information

Online prices, stock, coupons, delivery charges and promotional conditions may change at any time. A displayed price should not be treated as a guaranteed final purchase price. Always check the retailer's product page and checkout total before completing a purchase.

About DealRadar India

DealRadar India is an independent project focused on building better tools for online deal discovery in India.

Our mission

The long-term goal is to help shoppers notice meaningful opportunities that can be difficult to find manually, especially when prices change quickly.

What we are building

We are developing technology for automated deal discovery, price monitoring and useful shopping alerts. Availability and supported marketplaces may expand over time.

Coming next

DealRadar is currently being developed. Future features may include automated price monitoring, low-price discovery, historical price context and configurable deal alerts.
