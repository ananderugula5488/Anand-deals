About DealRadar India

Helping shoppers discover better opportunities.

DealRadar India is an independent project focused on making online deal discovery more useful, transparent and easier to understand.

What is DealRadar India?

DealRadar India is being developed as an online shopping information platform. The project focuses on discovering interesting price movements, offers and product opportunities across Indian online marketplaces.

Our goal

Our goal is to help shoppers identify potentially useful opportunities without relying only on large discount percentages. Price, availability, product information and purchasing conditions can all affect whether an offer is actually useful.

How we approach deals

A low advertised price does not automatically mean that a product represents good value. DealRadar is designed around the idea that price information should be considered together with product details, availability and other relevant conditions.

Independent project

DealRadar India is an independent project. Retailers control their own product listings, prices, inventory, delivery charges and promotional conditions. Information can therefore change without notice.

Important limitation

DealRadar does not guarantee that a displayed price, offer or product will remain available. Shoppers should always verify the current product page and final checkout price on the relevant retailer's website before purchasing.
