Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

DealRadar India respects your privacy. This page explains the general information practices of this independent deal-discovery project.

1. Information We Collect

DealRadar India is designed primarily as an informational website. We do not require visitors to create an account simply to browse the website.

If you voluntarily contact us, we may receive the information that you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, message or other contact information.

2. How Information May Be Used

Information voluntarily provided by visitors may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and feedback.
  • Handle business or project enquiries.
  • Improve the website and its content.
  • Understand and address reported issues.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

DealRadar India may use cookies or similar technologies if third-party services, analytics tools or other website features require them.

The availability and behaviour of such technologies may depend on the services integrated into the website at a particular time.

4. Third-Party Websites

DealRadar India may provide links to external retailer websites or other third-party services.

Once you leave DealRadar India and visit a third-party website, that website's own privacy policy and terms may apply. We do not control the privacy practices of external websites.

5. Deal and Product Information

DealRadar India may display information about products, prices, offers or availability obtained from or relating to external retailers and marketplaces.

Such information can change without notice. Visitors should verify current product details, prices, availability and retailer terms directly with the relevant retailer before making a purchase.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to this project to avoid unnecessary collection and disclosure of information.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information on the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

DealRadar India is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of creating accounts or profiles.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features, services or information practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the DealRadar India website, please contact us through the Contact page.
